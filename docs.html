Paprika docs

Everything Paprika can do and how to use it. New here? Start with Getting started, then look up any mod below.

Getting started

Install Fabric Loader and Fabric API for your Minecraft version, then put the Paprika jar for that version in your .minecraft/mods folder. The download page has the step by step guide.

Opening the menu

Press Right Shift or Delete in game. Press it again or Esc to close it. You can change these keys in Minecraft's Controls settings.

Turning mods on and changing settings

  • Menu style: click a mod to turn it on (green) or off (blue). The pencil opens its settings, the box next to it sets a keybind.
  • List style: left click a mod to turn it on, right click it to open its settings, description and keybind.
  • Start typing anywhere in the menu to search all mods.
  • Everything is saved straight away in .minecraft/config/paprika.json.

Keybinds

Click the keybind box, then press a key. You can hold Shift, Ctrl or Alt with it, for example Shift+R: the keybind is set when you let go. Esc while binding removes the keybind. Pressing the keybind in game turns the mod on or off.

Hacked client, not a ghost client

Paprika is a hacked client. A ghost client only has a few hidden mods made to look like a normal player. Paprika is built to do a lot, out in the open: flying, Xray, kill auras that hit through walls, Baritone and more.

That means many of its mods can be detected by anticheats (like Vulcan, Grim or Matrix) and can get you kicked or banned on servers that don't allow them. Paprika tries to stay as close to normal play as it can: attacks are real clicks, AimAssist moves like a mouse, and you can add a random delay to hits. Even so, no part of it is guaranteed to be undetectable.

  • Safest: singleplayer, your own server, and anarchy or cracked servers that allow clients.
  • Risky: servers with an anticheat. Mods like Fly, Speed, Noclip, Obvious kill aura and Reach are easy to spot there. Legit modes, low settings and a random delay help, but don't make you safe.
  • Mods marked May not work on servers are the ones servers usually block or undo.

Baritone

Baritone is a bot that walks, digs, bridges and pillars for you. Turn on the Baritone mod (Movement tab), then type these commands in chat. The first time, a tutorial page shows them too.

#b goto x y z
Walks to those coordinates.
#b goto [player/entity]
Walks to that player or mob.
#b gotomarker [name]
Walks to one of your markers (press M to make markers).
#b follow [player/entity]
Keeps following that player or mob until you use #b stop.
#b stop
Stops whatever Baritone is doing.
#b mine [block]
Finds the closest block of that type, mines it and keeps going until you stop it.
#b minea
Mines everything inside your areas (make areas with #a).
#b fillawith [block]
Fills your areas with a block from your inventory.
#b farm
Plants seeds on all farmland within 64 blocks, grows them with bone meal, harvests, collects and repeats.
#b farm [mob]
Goes to the nearest mob of that type, kills it and picks up the loot. Several at once: #b farm cow, pig, chicken.
#b avoidDanger
On or off: Baritone first kills hostile mobs that come close, then continues.
#b autoEat
On or off: Baritone eats when you get hungry during a task.
#b tutorial
Opens the Baritone tutorial page again.

Good to know

  • To bridge and pillar it needs throwaway blocks in your inventory: cobblestone, dirt, netherrack or planks.
  • It never digs into lava or water, also not through sand or gravel that has lava on top.
  • It picks up what it mines before going on.
  • If it gets stuck going back and forth, it tries another way, and gives up after a few tries.

Presets

A preset is a saved setup: which mods are on, every mod's settings and your keybinds. Make one for PvP, one for base hunting, and switch with one click.

  • Open the Presets tab (Menu style) or the Presets page (List style).
  • New preset saves how everything is set right now. Type a name and click OK.
  • Click a preset to use it. The one in use is green. The red trash can deletes it.
  • Friends, markers, HUD layout and theme aren't part of a preset. They stay the same.
  • Presets are files in .minecraft/config/paprika/configs/, so you can share them.

HUD editor

Move every Paprika HUD piece (watermark, FPS, coordinates, potion and armor HUD, the PlayerW list and more) wherever you want.

  • Open it with the HUD tab in the List style, the HUD Editor button in the pause menu or on the title screen.
  • Drag pieces with the left mouse button. Right click puts a piece back in its normal place.
  • Every piece looks the way it does in game, also mods that are off, so you can place them before turning them on. Pieces with nothing to show yet (no players near, no potion effects) show an example.
  • Esc saves and closes.

Friends

Add friends with #friend add [name] or on the Friends page of the List style. The kill auras, TriggerBot, AimAssist, CrystalAura, WebAura, BowAimbot, AutoLogout and PlayerW leave friends alone, ESP shows them in aqua and FriendW tells you when they're near.

Markers and areas

Markers: press M to open the marker menu. Give a marker a name, a color and coordinates. It shows on your screen with its distance, even through walls, and Baritone can walk to it with #b gotomarker [name]. In the List tab, the eye next to a marker hides or shows it (open eye: shown, closed eye: hidden) and the red trash can deletes it.

Areas: draw a box with #a. Baritone can mine everything inside with #b minea or fill it with #b fillawith [block].

Accounts

Play under another name on singleplayer and cracked (offline-mode) servers. Click Cracked in the top right of the Singleplayer or Multiplayer screen, make an account with New account and click it to use it (it turns green). Exit cracked takes you back to your Microsoft account. Online servers need your real account.

Troubleshooting

Minecraft says Paprika needs a different Minecraft version

You have the jar for another version. On the Install page every file says which Minecraft versions it's for: 1.21 and 1.21.1, or 1.20 and 1.20.1.

Minecraft says Fabric API is missing

Download Fabric API for your Minecraft version and put it in the same mods folder.

It crashes saying the Java version is wrong

Paprika for 1.20 and 1.20.1 needs Java 17, for 1.21 and 1.21.1 Java 21. The official launcher picks the right one for you; in other launchers, set the Java version in the profile settings.

The menu doesn't open

Another mod may use Right Shift or Delete too. Change Paprika's menu key in Controls.

A mod does nothing on a server

Many servers block movement and combat mods or move you back. Try it in singleplayer: if it works there, the server is stopping it.

Something else is wrong

Ask in the Discord and send the file .minecraft/logs/latest.log.
